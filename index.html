Publications

RANLP 2025 2025

A Culturally-Rich Romanian NLP Dataset from "Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?" Videos

Alexandru-Gabriel Ganea, Antonia-Adelina Popovici, Adrian-Marius Dumitran

Introduces a novel multilingual dataset derived from video recordings of the Romanian game show Vrei sa fii Milionar?, built using OCR, automated text extraction, and manual verification. Benchmarking state-of-the-art LLMs revealed significant performance gaps between international questions (80-95% accuracy) and Romanian-specific cultural questions (50-75%), highlighting the impact of cultural context on LLM evaluation.
